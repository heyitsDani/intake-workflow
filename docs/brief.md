Scenario Brief:
Task: The Intake Triage Agent

A professional services firm receives 40-60 inbound enquiries per week via a web form. Each enquiry includes a short free-text description of what the client needs, their industry, company size, and urgency. Today, a junior analyst reads every submission, tags it by service line, estimates complexity (simple/moderate/complex), and routes it to the right team lead. This takes roughly 8 hours per week and is error-prone.

Your job: design and prototype an automated triage system that classifies, enriches, and routes these enquiries - replacing the manual step.

Deliverables: A functioning prototype of the core classification/routing logic — the most basic version that covers the vital functionality. Notebook, small app, orchestration workflow — whatever you'd actually reach for. Use synthetic data.

Supplementary (optional) evidence to strengthen your case:
1. Architecture document (1-2 pages or a diagram + notes): How would you build this end-to-end? Cover data flow, model/API choices, integration points, and what you'd instrument from day one.
2. Brief write-up (half a page): What would break first in production? What would you monitor? What's your fallback when the model gets it wrong?
